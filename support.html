Support

MediaVerse Studio is preparing for its first public release and a beta test. Questions, ideas and problem reports are welcome at contact@mediaverse.studio.

When reporting a problem, it helps to say which version you use (shown in Settings > About), which device (PC, phone, TV model), what you did, and what happened. Once the source code is public, problems can also be reported on the project's GitHub page.

Joining the beta

We would like testers on Windows 11, Android phones (with or without Android Auto), Samsung smart TVs, Google TV and Chromecast devices, and homes with a Jellyfin server; Linux users are welcome to say hello for when that build exists. Write to contact@mediaverse.studio with the devices you have and how big your collection is.

  • Windows: we send you the installer. Windows may show a "protected your PC" screen because the installer is not signed yet (signing is part of going public); choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • Android: we add you to a closed test on Google Play. Google asks testers to stay opted in for 14 days and to open the app now and then; that is what lets the app be published for everyone.
  • Samsung TV: the TV app is installed in developer mode for now, and we help you do it; the store version comes after review.
  • What we ask: use it the way you normally listen and tell us what happened, good or bad. You never need to run tests by rote, and nothing is collected from your computer.

Supporting the project

MediaVerse Studio is free and will stay free. It is supported only by voluntary donations; a donation link will be added here once it is set up.

Thanks

  • MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive
  • AcoustID
  • LRCLIB
  • gnudb
  • Jellyfin, the free media server that MediaVerse Studio can share its playlists and flagged songs with
  • AccurateRip
