Support

MediaVerse Studio is still in development and has not been released yet. Questions, ideas and problem reports are welcome at contact@mediaverse.studio.

When reporting a problem, it helps to say which version you use (shown in Settings), what you did, and what happened.

Supporting the project

MediaVerse Studio is free and will stay free. It is supported only by voluntary donations; a donation link will be added here once it is set up.

Thanks

MediaVerse Studio builds on the generosity of these open music services and projects:

  • MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive
  • AcoustID
  • LRCLIB
  • gnudb
  • AccurateRip
  • libcdio, LAME and many open-source libraries
