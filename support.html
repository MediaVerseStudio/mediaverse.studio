Support

MediaVerse Studio is in public beta. Questions, ideas and problem reports are best posted on the project's GitHub issue board: you can see what is already known, others can help, and nothing gets lost in an inbox.

When reporting a problem, it helps to say which version you use (shown in Settings > About), which device (PC, phone, TV model), what you did, and what happened. Search the board first in case someone has already reported it.

Joining the beta

We would like testers on Windows 11, Android phones (with or without Android Auto), Samsung smart TVs, Google TV and Chromecast devices, and homes with a Jellyfin server; Linux users are welcome to say hello for when that build exists. Download the beta and tell us how it goes on the issue board; the feedback form asks for your devices and how big your collection is. To join the Google Play closed test we need the Google account address you use on your phone, which should not be posted in public: please e-mail contact@mediaverse.studio for that one.

  • Windows: we send you the installer. Windows may show a "protected your PC" screen because the installer is not signed yet (signing is part of going public); choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • Android: we add you to a closed test on Google Play. Google asks testers to stay opted in for 14 days and to open the app now and then; that is what lets the app be published for everyone.
  • Samsung TV: the TV app is installed in developer mode for now, and we help you do it; the store version comes after review.
  • What we ask: use it the way you normally listen and tell us what happened, good or bad. You never need to run tests by rote, and nothing is collected from your computer.

Supporting the project

MediaVerse Studio is free and will stay free. It is supported only by voluntary donations; a donation link will be added here once it is set up.

Thanks

MediaVerse Studio builds on the generosity of these open music services and projects:

  • MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive
  • AcoustID
  • LRCLIB
  • gnudb
  • Jellyfin, the free media server that MediaVerse Studio can share its playlists and flagged songs with
  • AccurateRip
  • libcdio, LAME and many open-source libraries
