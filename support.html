Support

MediaVerse Studio is in public beta. Questions, ideas and problem reports are best posted on the project's GitHub issue board: you can see what is already known, others can help, and nothing gets lost in an inbox.

When reporting a problem, it helps to say which version you use (shown in Settings > About), which device (PC, phone, TV model), what you did, and what happened. Search the board first in case someone has already reported it.

Joining the beta

We would like testers on Windows 11, Android phones (with or without Android Auto), Samsung smart TVs, Google TV and Chromecast devices, and homes with a Jellyfin server; Linux users are welcome to say hello and ask to try the first test builds. Download the beta and tell us how it goes on the issue board; the feedback form asks for your devices and how big your collection is. To join the Google Play closed test when it opens, we need the Google account address you use on your phone, which should not be posted in public: please e-mail contact@mediaverse.studio for that one.

  • Windows: download the installer. Windows may show a "protected your PC" screen because the installer is not signed yet; choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • Android: download the .apk today. For the Google Play closed test later, Google asks testers to stay opted in for 14 days and to open the app now and then; that is what lets the app be published for everyone.
  • Samsung TV: the TV app is installed in developer mode for now; the store version comes after review.
  • What we ask: use it the way you normally listen and tell us what happened, good or bad. You never need to run tests by rote, and nothing is collected from your computer.

Supporting the services behind it

MediaVerse Studio is free and will stay free. It is made in spare time and has no donation link: the best support is to try the beta and tell us what you find on the issue board. The disc, album, cover and lyric details the program fills in come from services run by individuals and non-profits who depend on gifts. If you would like to give something back, please consider them, in the order the project's owner would put them:

  • LRCLIB: a free, nonprofit community lyrics database (its own words), and where the program finds most time-synced lyrics. Its home page has a Support LRCLIB button with ways to give, including a monthly goal.
  • gnudb: a free, advertising-free CD database operated by an individual (its own words) and kept going by donations; its home page says donations do not yet cover its costs. It is the second opinion after MusicBrainz when you rip a disc.
  • Jellyfin: the volunteer-built media server this program works with; its site says its costs are paid through donations from users.
  • MusicBrainz and the MetaBrainz Foundation: the open music encyclopedia behind the disc and album lookups, run by a non-profit.
  • Wikimedia: Wikimedia Commons is where artist photos come from.
  • Internet Archive: stores the Cover Art Archive pictures the program finds for your albums.

The same list is in the program, under Settings > About.
