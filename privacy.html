Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026

MediaVerse Studio is a free, non-commercial project. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not collect information about you. Your music, your library and your settings stay on your own devices.

The Windows program

Your library, favorites, flags, listening history and settings are kept in the program's data folder on your computer. Nothing is sent to us.

To look things up, the program contacts these public music services directly, only when you use a feature that needs them:

  • MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive: a disc's identifier or table of contents, or an album and artist name, to find album details and covers.
  • gnudb: a disc's identifier, to find album details. gnudb asks every program for a contact e-mail address; the program sends the one you enter in Settings.
  • AccurateRip: a disc's identifier, to check that a rip is exact.
  • LRCLIB: a song's title, artist, album and length, to find its lyrics.
  • AcoustID (being added): an audio fingerprint and a song's length, to recognise a song, using the application key you enter in Settings.
  • Discogs (being added; optional, only if you add your own Discogs access token): artist and album names, to find album details.

Each service receives your computer's internet address, as any website does, and handles it under its own privacy policy. MediaVerse Studio identifies itself to these services by name and version so that they can see which program is asking.

The TV app

The Samsung TV app talks to MediaVerse Studio on your own computer over your home network, after a one-time pairing code, or to a Jellyfin server you choose, or reads a USB drive plugged into the TV. Profiles and favorites made on the TV are kept on the TV. The app does not send anything to us.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and sets no cookies of its own. GitHub may log visitors' internet addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

E-mail

If you write to contact@mediaverse.studio, your message and address are used only to answer you, and are never shared or sold.

Changes

If the program's behaviour changes in a way that affects this page, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
